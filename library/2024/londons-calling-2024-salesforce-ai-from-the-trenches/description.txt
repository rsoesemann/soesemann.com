Since ChatGPT’s release, we at Aquiva Labs have been exploring AI in Salesforce, creating PoCs, free AppExchange apps, and open-source contributions. This session will share our journey, showcasing the tools we’ve developed, the methodologies we’ve adopted, and the lessons learned. Spoiler alert: Mastering custom AI is challenging, yet leveraging the support of a Low Code Platform is more vital than ever for pragmatic AI implementation.
