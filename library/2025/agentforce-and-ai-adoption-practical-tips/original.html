Agentforce and AI Adoption: Practical Tips

November 4, 2025
4:00 PM to 5:00 PM

Speakers

Peter Chittum

Technical Content Director

Robert Sösemann

Senior Principal Architect, Aquiva Labs

Melissa Hill Dees

Technical Evangelist and Partner Engagement, EzProtect

As AI moves from hype to hands-on value, businesses are asking: what’s real, what’s next, and how do we actually make it work? Join Salesforce Ben’s expert panel for a candid conversation on navigating AI adoption with confidence and clarity.

What to Expect:

  • A grounded look at where we stand on the AI hype curve – and what’s beyond it.
  • Insights into the real obstacles slowing AI adoption and how to overcome them.
  • Practical guidance on identifying high-impact AI use cases that deliver results.

Discover how to innovate amidst rapid technological change and chart your own path toward meaningful AI adoption. Reserve your spot today to join the discussion!
