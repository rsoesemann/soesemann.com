What you'll learn
Play by Play is a series in which top technologists work through a problem in real time, unrehearsed, and unscripted. In this course, Play by Play: Sipping the Happy Soup - Untangle Production Orgs with Salesforce DX Unlocked Packages, Salesforce Architect Robert Sösemann will bust common myths that Salesforce DX is only helpful for ISVs using Managed Packages. Starting from a real world scenario he will guide us in small riskless steps through the process of converting a Happy Soup Org full of tangled Metadata into a set of well-defined packages that can be versioned and worked on independently by different teams.
