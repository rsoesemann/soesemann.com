Salesforce Play by Play is an interactive series on Pluralsight.com, where we sit down with Salesforce experts to discuss common challenges faced every day by Salesforce customers.

In this course, Robert Sösemann and Don Robins will bust common myths that Salesforce DX is only helpful for ISVs using Managed Packages. Starting from a real-world scenario Robert will guide us in small riskless steps through the process of converting a Happy Soup Org full of tangled Metadata into a set of well-defined packages that can be versioned and worked on independently by different teams.
